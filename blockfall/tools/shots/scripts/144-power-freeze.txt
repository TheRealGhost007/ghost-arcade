#! env SHOTS_SETUP=4,0,10,20
# Freeze (3.1.0): an ice wash crosses the well, frost grows in from the
# corners over 0.6 s and holds; in the last 1.5 s it blinks (the bar in the
# panel with it), and when it ends the frost breaks with a thaw tick.
wait 10
tap ENTER
wait 2
tap ENTER
wait 2
game clear
game row 0 ####.#####
game row 1 #.#######.
game row 2 ###.####..
game piece O 0 6
game power Freeze 0
wait 2
mark sounds
tapshot SPACE 144-freeze-f1
expect sounds jingle:COIN,sfx:11
expect hud callouts FREEZE
expect feel frost 0.05
wait 5
shot 144-freeze-f7
wait 14
shot 144-freeze-f22
wait 20
shot 144-freeze-f43
expect feel frost 1.00
# the top-left frost (its second stair, under the ice frame), lit
expect pixels 44 60 16 2 above 120
# the last 1.5 s: it blinks (4 Hz)
game freeze 1.4
shot 144-freeze-blink-dim
expect pixels 44 60 16 2 below 100
wait 4
shot 144-freeze-blink-lit
expect pixels 44 60 16 2 above 120
wait 7
shot 144-freeze-blink-dim-again
expect pixels 44 60 16 2 below 100
# the end: the frost breaks, with its tick
game freeze 0.03
mark sounds
wait 3
shot 144-freeze-thaw
expect feel power thaw
expect feel bits >0
expect sounds sfx:12
wait 20
expect feel power none
