#! env SHOTS_ADMIN=0 SHOTS_HIDDEN=rockdrift
# Phase 5: Settings > Machines for a player: the status of every machine
# (hidden ones too), the row's own buttons, and no Add, Edit or Remove. An
# install from a machine's page shows as Installing.
wait 30
tap TAB
wait 3
tap SHIFT TAB
wait 5
expect app tab Machines
expect app look:Add a machine absent
expect app look:Edit absent
expect app look:Remove absent
expect app status:Rockdrift Installed, hidden
mouse 900 600
wait 3
shot machines-player
tap ESCAPE
wait 5
# Moondrop's page (the row's fifth, Rockdrift being hidden): Install.
tap PAGE_DOWN
tap LEFT
wait 30
expect app lit Moondrop
tap I
wait 8
tap ENTER
wait 5
expect count 1 install Moondrop
tap ESCAPE
wait 5
tap TAB
wait 5
expect app tab Machines
expect app status:Moondrop Installing
shot machines-installing
install fail
wait 200
expect app status:Moondrop Not installed
