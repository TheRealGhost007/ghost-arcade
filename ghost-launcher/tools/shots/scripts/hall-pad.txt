#! env SHOTS_ADMIN=0
# Phase 3: the hall on the Xbox pad, the owner's map: A play, Y daily,
# X this game (its page), View stats, Menu or B
# the Arcade menu, LB/RB five machines along, the d-pad (or stick) one along
# with repeat. The strip then names the pad's buttons, drawn in the house
# colours.
wait 30
pad on
pad tap RIGHT
wait 30
expect selected 1
expect app hint 1
expect app chip:Play A normal
expect app chip:Daily Y disabled
expect app chip:This_game X normal
expect app chip:Stats View normal
expect app chip:Menu Menu normal
expect app status Press A to play
wait 10
shot hall-strip-pad

# LB/RB: five along, stopping at the ends, then round.
pad tap RB
wait 3
expect selected 6
pad tap RB
wait 3
expect selected 11
pad tap RB
wait 3
expect selected 0
pad tap LB
wait 3
expect selected 11
pad tap LB
wait 3
expect selected 6
# The d-pad held keeps moving.
pad hold LEFT 40
wait 3
expect selected 2

# X: this machine's page. View: Stats. Menu and B: the Arcade menu.
pad tap X
wait 5
expect ui GAME
expect app pagefor Brickburst
pad tap B
wait 3
expect ui LIST
pad tap VIEW
wait 5
expect ui STATS
pad tap VIEW
wait 3
expect ui LIST
pad tap MENU
wait 5
expect ui MENU
pad tap MENU
wait 3
expect ui LIST
pad tap B
wait 5
expect ui MENU
pad tap B
wait 3
expect ui LIST

# Y: the daily, on Brickburst (it has one).
expect selected 2
pad tap Y
wait 70
expect launched Brickburst --daily
gameexit
wait 5
# Y on Coilrush, which has none: the card says so.
pad tap LEFT
wait 30
pad tap Y
wait 3
expect app note Coilrush has no daily challenge.
expect launches 1
# A plays exactly once.
pad tap A
wait 70
expect launched Coilrush
expect launches 2
gameexit
wait 5

# A nudge of the mouse keeps the pad's buttons on the strip; a key turns
# them back to keys.
mouse 480 450
wait 3
expect app chip:Play A normal
tap RIGHT
wait 3
expect app chip:Play Enter normal
expect app chip:Daily D normal
