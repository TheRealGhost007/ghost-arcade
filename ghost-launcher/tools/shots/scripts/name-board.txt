# Phase 3: the pad's name board. The pad reaches Settings > Player through
# the Arcade menu; A on Edit brings an arcade high-score letter grid up
# under the name. The d-pad moves round it, A types, X deletes, Y types a
# space, Menu saves, B puts the old name back. The mouse clicks its keys;
# the keyboard still types into the name.
wait 30
pad on
pad tap MENU
wait 5
expect ui MENU
pad tap DOWN
pad tap DOWN
wait 2
pad tap A
wait 5
expect ui SETTINGS
expect app tab Player
expect app focus Edit
pad tap A
wait 5
expect app typing 1
expect app board 1
expect app boardcell A
expect profile TRG
shot name-board

pad tap A
wait 2
expect profile TRGA
pad tap LEFT
wait 2
expect app boardcell M
pad tap A
wait 2
expect profile TRGAM
pad tap X
wait 2
expect profile TRGA
pad tap Y
pad tap DOWN
pad tap DOWN
pad tap A
wait 2
expect app boardcell _
expect profile TRGA _

wait 2
expect app boardcell Done
pad tap LEFT
pad tap A
wait 2
expect app boardcell Del

pad tap UP
wait 2
expect app boardcell 5
